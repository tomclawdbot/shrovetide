SHROVETIDE

Carry the stone to the other side's millstone and hold it for three taps.

How to play

  • Run — WASD / stick. Sprint — Shift / Sprint pad (costs Breath).
  • Kick — hold Space / Kick. Rip a trapped stone — hold F / Wriggle·Rip in the hug.
  • Goal — at their millstone with the stone, tap E / Goal three times.
  • Hug packs only around the stone; once it breaks free, the scrum peels apart.

Breath

  • Moving, sprinting, carrying, and ripping all spend Breath.
  • Breath returns only while you stand still — not while jogging.
  • Spent Breath (empty bar) leaves you crawling until you rest.
